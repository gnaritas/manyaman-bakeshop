Our Story

A Filipino bake shop
lands in Vernon.

Man Yaman Bake Shop opened in November 2026 at 101 West Street, bringing traditional Filipino baked goods, cakes, cookies, savory pastries and desserts — flavors and recipes rooted in Filipino cuisine — to Vernon, CT.

To the Neighbors Who Waited

“Thank you for choosing us, for waiting for us, and for showing us more love than we could have ever imagined. We see you. We appreciate you. And we are truly grateful for you.”

— Your ManYaman Family, in a letter to the community after opening week

The welcome was overwhelming from the very first morning — lines formed, trays emptied, and the shop answered with a thank-you letter to the neighborhood. The baking stays the same: pandesal at dawn, kakanin made properly, and cakes for the moments that matter.
