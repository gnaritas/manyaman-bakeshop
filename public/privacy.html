What This Site Collects

This site has one form. When you send a message, the name, phone number, email address and message you type are delivered to Man Yaman Bake Shop so we can get back to you. That is the only personal information this site handles.

What Happens to It

Messages are used to answer your question — about orders, the menu, hours, or anything else. We do not sell or rent the information you send. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.
